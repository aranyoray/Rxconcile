# Rxconcile Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 19, 2026_

Rxconcile is designed around a simple principle: **your health information stays
on your device.** This policy explains what the app does and does not do with
your data.

## Summary

- We do **not** collect, sell, or transmit your personal or health information.
- Your medication list is stored **only on your device**.
- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
- Camera and microphone are used **on-device** and nothing is uploaded.

## Information the app stores (on your device only)

When you add a medication, Rxconcile stores the details you enter (name, dosage,
packaging, quantity, expiration, reminder schedule, and an optional donor name)
in the app's private storage on your device, protected by iOS file encryption.
This data is never sent to us or any third party. Deleting the app removes it.

## Camera and label scanning

If you scan a medication label, the photo is processed **on your device** using
Apple's on-device text recognition to read the drug name, NDC, and expiration.
The image is **not saved** by the app and **not uploaded** anywhere. We encourage
you to black out personal information on the label before scanning.

## Microphone and voice features

If you enable voice commands, speech is processed using Apple's on-device speech
recognition where available. Spoken reminders are produced by your device's
built-in text-to-speech. Audio is **not recorded, stored, or transmitted** by us.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device through iOS notifications. They do
not involve any server.

## Links to external resources

The app may link you to third-party resources such as licensed medication
repositories (e.g. SIRUM) or government take-back locators (e.g. the DEA
locator). Those sites have their own privacy policies; we do not control them.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect accounts, contacts, location history, advertising identifiers,
or usage analytics. Our App Store privacy disclosure reflects **"Data Not
Collected,"** consistent with the bundled privacy manifest.

## Children

Rxconcile is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Medical disclaimer

Rxconcile provides general informational guidance only. It does not provide
medical advice and does not facilitate person-to-person transfer of medication.
Donation eligibility is always determined by a licensed pharmacist or repository.
Always follow the guidance of your healthcare provider and applicable law.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post
