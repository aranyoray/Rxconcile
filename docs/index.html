Rxconcile Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Rxconcile is designed around a simple principle: your health information stays on your device.

Summary

  • We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal or health information.
  • Your medication list is stored only on your device.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.
  • Camera and microphone are used on-device; nothing is uploaded.

Information the app stores (on your device only)

Medication details you enter are stored in the app's private, encrypted on-device storage. This data is never sent to us or any third party. Deleting the app removes it.

Camera and label scanning

Label photos are processed on-device with Apple's text recognition to read the drug name, NDC, and expiration. The image is not saved and not uploaded. Please black out personal information before scanning.

Microphone and voice features

Voice commands use Apple's on-device speech recognition where available, and spoken reminders use your device's text-to-speech. Audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted by us.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no server is involved.

External links

The app may link to third-party resources (e.g. SIRUM, the DEA take-back locator) that have their own privacy policies.

Information we do not collect

No accounts, contacts, location history, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. Our App Store disclosure reflects "Data Not Collected."

Medical disclaimer

Rxconcile provides general informational guidance only, does not provide medical advice, and does not facilitate person-to-person transfer of medication. Eligibility is determined by a licensed pharmacist or repository.
